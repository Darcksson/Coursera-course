Discover the History of Mexico City

Mexico City is one of the cultural capitals of the world, home to over 150 museums that preserve centuries of art, tradition, and heritage. From ancient pre-Hispanic civilizations to the modern era, walking through its museums is the best way to understand the identity of Mexico. Explore two of its most iconic historical landmarks below.

Templo Mayor Museum

Step into the heart of the ancient Aztec Empire. Located right next to the Zócalo, this museum showcases the archaeological ruins of the main temple of Tenochtitlan and thousands of artifacts uncovered in the center of the city.

National History Museum

Perched atop Chapultepec Hill, the only royal castle in North America houses a vast collection of murals, historical documents, and royal artifacts that tell the story of Mexico from the Spanish conquest to the Mexican Revolution.
